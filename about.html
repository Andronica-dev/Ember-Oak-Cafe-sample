Our Story

Built Around Good Coffee & Good People

Ember & Oak Café began with a simple idea: create a welcoming space where people could enjoy great coffee, fresh food and meaningful moments together.

Our goal is to continue growing into a place that our community can rely on — whether it's for your morning coffee, a weekend brunch, or simply a quiet moment to yourself.

We're always looking for ways to improve, grow and create an even better experience for everyone who walks through our doors.
